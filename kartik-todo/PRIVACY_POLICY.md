# Bloom Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 13 September 2026

Bloom is an offline-first task and reminder application developed by Kartik.

## Data Bloom stores
Bloom stores the following information locally on the user's device:
- the name entered during first-time setup;
- tasks, dates, times, completion state, recurrence settings and reminder preferences;
- light/dark theme preference;
- reminder style preference; and
- the URI/reference of a device sound selected for alarm-style reminders.

## Data collection and sharing
Bloom 1.1.0 does not include an account system, advertising SDK, analytics SDK or third-party tracking SDK. Bloom does not transmit the user's task list or profile information to a Bloom server.

## Permissions
Bloom may request notification permission so it can display task reminders. Reminder scheduling uses Android's local alarm APIs. Bloom requests the boot-completed permission only so one-time and recurring reminders can be restored locally after the device restarts. If the user chooses a sound from the device, Android's system picker is used.

## Recurring reminders
Bloom supports one-time, daily, weekly and yearly tasks. Recurrence rules, task times and reminder offsets are stored locally and used only to schedule reminders on the device.

## Data retention and deletion
The data above remains on the device until the user edits/deletes it, clears Bloom's app storage, or uninstalls Bloom. Clearing app storage or uninstalling the application removes Bloom's locally stored app data from that device.

## Children
Bloom is a general productivity application and is not specifically directed to children.

## Changes
This policy may be updated when Bloom's features or data practices change. The effective date at the top will be updated accordingly.

## Contact
The developer contact email should be the same support email supplied in the Google Play Console listing.
